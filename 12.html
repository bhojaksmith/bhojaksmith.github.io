Getting started

From setup to everyday attendance.

Four stages take a school from first setup to a daily routine that runs on its own.

1
2
3
4
STAGE 1
School setup
Set up your school in Good Morning, along with the students and staff who will mark attendance.
STAGE 2
Device setup
Prepare compatible Android devices, running Android 12 or later, at entrances or in classrooms.
STAGE 3
Everyday attendance
Students and staff mark attendance by face. Each record is saved digitally with the date and time.
STAGE 4
Review and use records
The office reviews attendance and working hours, and uses staff records in payroll workflows.
Device requirement · Android 12 or later. Book a demo to walk through setup for your school
