03 · Staff Attendance

Every clock-in counts.

Bring staff attendance and working-hour records into one digital workflow.

Teachers and non-teaching staff mark attendance by face when they arrive and leave. Each clock-in and clock-out is recorded, so working hours are available without a separate register.
